gfql_semantic_zeek_hunt_sample.json.gz is a subset of the Zeek logs in
https://github.com/brimdata/zed-sample-data (zeek-json: http.json.gz and conn.json.gz).
Those logs come from the WRCCDC 2018-03-24 packet capture archives (https://www.wrccdc.org/).

the web requests and a reduced list of connection pairs, renames fields and converts times to
milliseconds.
